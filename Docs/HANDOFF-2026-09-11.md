## ⭐ THE METHOD — and the two things it overturned

**⭐ A RECORDED CAUSE IS A HYPOTHESIS UNTIL SOMETHING MEASURES IT, AND TWO OF THIS REPO'S
WERE WRONG.**

- **`lineThickness`'s note said "a thickened stem moves its own far edge, which is where the
  beam is anchored".** Reduced to one bar — `(3ceg|` at `L:1/8` — and swept over
  `lineThickness` 0, 0.5, 1.5 and 3, abcjs's tuplet number sits at `y=89.33` at **every**
  value while ours moves to 87.47. **abcjs never moves ANYTHING for `lineThickness`**: it is

  moved too.
- **The `wrap` row's note said the 60 split into line-count and geometry.** True, and the

**⭐ AND THE NARROW FIX THAT FOLLOWS FROM A CORRECT READING CAN STILL BE WRONG.** abcjs's

## 🔬 WHAT LANDED

**`wrap` — ELEVEN LINE-COUNT DEFECTS TO ZERO.** A break indexes **BAR ELEMENTS**, not
measures: `getMeasureWidths` reports one width per bar (`engraver-controller.js:167-180`) and
`findLineBreaks` counts the same (`wrap_lines.js:132-141`), so a measure that OPENS with a
barline contributes TWO where an ordinary one contributes one — the width array is
`measures + openingBarlines`:

    |:CDEF|1GABc:|3cdef|]    3 measures, 1 opening   ->  4 widths
    visual-tablature-17      5 measures, 5 openings  -> 10 widths
    synth-flattener-46      17 measures, 3 openings  -> 20 widths

⚠️ **And which measure a break opens depends on which of the two it lands on** — a CLOSING
bar opens the next measure, an OPENING bar opens that one. ⚠️ **A first fix that treated it
as a single leading offset per section closed one fixture of eleven**: the offset accumulates
measure by measure, so only a walk of the bar elements gets it right.

**`tune.lines` WAS THE PARSED STRUCTURE WHERE abcjs RE-LINES IT.** `wrapLines` rewrites the
tune (`parse/wrap_lines.js:13`); an eight-bar tune under `{wrap, staffwidth: 400}` DRAWS three
lines in both engines while `tune.lines` reported three in abcjs and ONE here. **No gate here
could see it** — the drawing was already right.

**`add_classes` — 17 to 16, on three class rules.** The two callers differ by ONE ARGUMENT:
`addMultiLine`'s array branch opens a group with the klass and hands `richText` a literal
`''`, which `if (klass)` leaves UNSET — so the row serialises as the literal
`class="undefined"` — while `addSingleLine` has no group and passes its klass through. A
BOXED row's group carries the class before its fill. And a GENERATED class beats the field's
own, because `classes.generate` appends the line counter.
