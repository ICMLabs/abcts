### 1. AN ABOVE DYNAMIC IS DRAWN AT A FIXED STEP — measured, not fixed

`ENGRAVE.dynamicAboveStep: 19.5` is the LAST lane constant that does not cancel. `partStep`,
`tempoStep`, `lyricStep`, `annotationAboveStep` and `dynamicBelowStep` are reference points
their anchor pass shifts FROM — `anchorAboveStaff`, `anchorLyrics` and `anchorBelowStaff`
place chord symbols, annotations, part labels, tempo marks, lyrics and BELOW dynamics on the
music's own ink. **Nothing places an ABOVE dynamic.**

Four controls, all with a `w:` line so abcjs puts dynamics above (`hasVocals`,
`decoration.js:379`):

```
              staff top       top line y      the MARK
CDEF          13.7244 exact   64.12 exact     —
!mf!CDEF      20.7244 exact   64.12 exact     abcjs starts y 29.15, ours y -0.07
!mf!c'DEF     22.0444 exact   69.23 exact     abcjs starts y 29.15, ours y  5.05
c'DEF         15.0444 exact   69.23 exact     —
```

The staff EXTENT is exact on all four. Ours is a CONSTANT 64.18px above the top line and
abcjs's is not — abcjs's mark stays at the same absolute y while the staff moves down under
it, which is what pinning to the top of the reserved lane looks like. On the second rung
ours lands at y ≈ 0 and is CLIPPED OFF THE PAGE.

`set-upper-and-lower-elements.js:39-46`: the lane goes on `staff.top`, then
`positionY.dynamicHeightAbove = staff.top` and the mark draws there.

**THE FIX IS THE ORDERING, NOT THE ARITHMETIC.** abcjs stacks above in the order chord,
ending, dynamic, part, tempo. Ours spends chord/part/tempo in `anchorAboveStaff` and the
ending and dynamic lanes in `verticalExtent`, so the staff's TOTAL is right either way and
only the mark's own y is wrong. `anchorBelowStaff` is the model for the cheap version — it
takes `verticalExtent(...).bottom` minus its own lane and shifts. The honest version moves
both lanes into `anchorAboveStaff`'s stack, which that function's own `ponytail:` note has
been asking for since finding 93. **Do the cheap one first and measure**: both ranked tables
are at 0, so any regression shows immediately.
