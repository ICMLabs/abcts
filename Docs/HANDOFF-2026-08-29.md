## WHAT LANDED, AND WHERE IT CAME FROM

### 1. ENUMERATING abcjs'S OWN DIRECTIVE SWITCH — 18 never mentioned here

⚠️ **SEVENTEEN WERE ALREADY BYTE-EXACT.** "Never mentioned in our parser" is not "not
implemented by abcjs", and enumerating the REFERENCE is what tells them apart.

- **`%%staffnonote 0` drops every staff holding nothing but rests**, and the sense of the
  boolean is INVERTED — abcjs's own comment says so. A rest carrying a CHORD SYMBOL keeps
  its staff; a bare rest does not; a tune whose every voice is rests keeps no staff at all
  and falls to the line-level deletion, which is what gets its 37.56px page right.
- **Five of the seventeen still warned `Unknown directive`** where abcjs knows them and says
  nothing (`%%papersize`, the four `%%-` info fields). The SVG was exact throughout — only
  the WARNINGS gate could see it.

### 2. TWO abcjs BUGS, BOTH RULED — `Docs/ABCJS-DIFFERENCES.md`

- ⚠️ **`%%beginps` WITH A NON-EMPTY BODY NEVER RETURNS.** Its loop advances the tokenizer
  and never reassigns the variable it tests (`abc_parse_directive.js:969-975`); `begintext`
  ten lines above is the same shape and ends its body with the assignment. Ours returns in
  5ms. **We decline to reproduce a hang** — there is no output to be byte-equal to.
- ⚠️ **`%%staffnonote 0` OVER PURE RESTS CRASHES `extractMeasures`.** Deleting every staff
  leaves `line.staff === []` — truthy — and it reads `line.staff[0].voices` under a
  hard-coded `k < 1` bound. Split into `abcts-staffnonote-empty-staves.abc` and named in a
  new `DIVERGENT` list on that gate. ⚠️ NOT in `STALE`, which means "the golden was not
  regenerated" and would hide a real regression.

### 3. THE `ponytail:` LEDGER — nine of ten predictions were already right
