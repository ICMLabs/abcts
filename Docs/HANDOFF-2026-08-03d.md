### 1b. WAS: mid-tune non-music lines

A `T:` subtitle or a `%%text` block standing BETWEEN two music lines is dropped. abcjs
draws each as a LINE of its own, so it pushes the systems below it down.

The machinery is already there and is the reason this is now worth doing: the top-text
block is built by `topTextBlock` and injected as a `type: 'title'` element carrying
`blockHeight`, which `verticalExtent` already accounts for. A mid-tune block wants exactly
the same treatment on the first staff of a LATER system, which means:

- the parser recording which system a block precedes (the free-text blocks are on the
  Score today; they need a system index, or a `textBefore` on the measure that opens the
  system after them);
- `topTextBlock`'s row logic reused for a block with no title.

`visual-options-01-fonts` (oy −44.6), the two `selection-test` (116.6), `mouse-click-01`
and `tablature-15` (69.8) all carry one, and `S2-fields` is the 41-corpus case.

**Careful:** a `T:` that turns out to be after ALL the music is drawn at the FOOT and moves
nothing — measured, `mouse-click-01`'s "Inserted subtitle" sits at y 630.21 below both
systems. Its `T:` is in the last VOICE of a system, not between systems.
