   / `alignVoiceMeasures`, `lineAbsent` placeholders), as abcjs's `tune.lines` does. MIDI
   tracks are counted per line with silence padding.
3. **The WRAPPED `tune.lines` got a gate** — `scripts/zzwraplines.mjs` + `-known.json`. It
   started at 529 of 697 differing.
4. **…and was closed by a PORT, not by rules.** abcjs builds it as
   `addLineBreaks(deline(lines), findLineBreaks(…))` over the tune as parsed. `deline` and
   `findLineBreaks` were already ported and the unwrapped projection was already exact, so
   porting `addLineBreaks` (`compat/wrap.ts`) and running the three stages
   (`compat/index.ts` `wrappedLines`) closed every row. **~330 lines of `lines.ts` that had
   been rebuilding the answer case by case were then proven dead and deleted.**

   tune-relative; a `%%staffnonote`-emptied line still opens a width section.

nine in one step and made all of those rules dead code. Before modelling a derived surface,
ask whether abcjs derives it from one we already match.
