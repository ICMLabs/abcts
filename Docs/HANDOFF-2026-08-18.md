### 1. THE OVERLAY MODEL — DONE (2026-08-19). `setupEvents` IS CLOSED

Every open `setupEvents` row and three of the open `makeVoicesArray` tunes were this one
mechanism, and it is fixed the way this file said it had to be: **the resolution is SHARED
rather than re-derived.** `resolveOverlays` moved to `src/core/overlays.ts` and the PARSER
now runs it over a line-structured view of the model (`padOverlays`), so the renderer, the
clock and the flattener lay out the voices a host reads. All three already expanded
`measure.overlays`; what changed is what is in it.

**setupEvents 3,345 → 3,366 of 3,366 (CLOSED). makeVoicesArray 4,153 → 4,167, 168 → 171 of
177 tunes. Both SVG byte gates stayed at zero.**

Five things it cost, each of which transfers:

- **THE PADDING IS TWO DIFFERENT THINGS AND WE WROTE ONE.** On the layer's own line a
  measure that does not sing takes ONE invisible rest of the measure's summed duration
  carrying the BARLINE's span (`tune-builder.js:572-575`); a line ABOVE one that sings is
  BACK-FILLED with one rest PER NOTE, each carrying that note's own span (`:536-545`). A
  single spanless per-measure rest is neither.
- **A LINE'S LAYER COUNT IS NOT THE TUNE'S MAXIMUM.** The back-fill's guard —
  `staff.voices.length < s.voices.length`, the CURRENT line's count against the earlier
  line's, taken BEFORE the new voice is pushed — is what stops a line already carrying its
  own layer from being back-filled by a later one. `synth-flattener-21` is 3, 3, 3, 2, 2
  voices where its maximum is 3, and our model now prints that shape element for element.
- ⚠️ **AN EMPTY PART IS NOT A FREE ONE.** Dropping the silent layer from the staff MERGE
  alone left it in the line SOLVE, where a voice with no staff-extra of its own still moved
  the left edge: the last two clefs stood at 20 against abcjs's 15 and the whole corpus
  differed by exactly those five pixels. It has to contribute no advances either.
- ⚠️ **AN ARRAY IS AN IDENTITY.** `stavesHere` recovers a staff with
  `voicesOfStaff.indexOf(members)`, so filtering the member list into a NEW array cost
  eleven byte-exact fixtures their brace — every one of them a tune with no `&` in it.
  Filter a COPY inside the merge instead.
- **THE TWO RESOLUTIONS ARE SEPARATE RUNS, SO A PAD HAS NO abcelem.** The model's pads are
  what the renderer lays out and the projection's are what a host reads, and no identity
  joins them — so `abcelemOf` SYNTHESIZES the element, with the projected span of the note
  it mirrors (`Rest.overlayMirrors`). The model's own range is the RAW one, which stops
  short of the trailing whitespace abcjs's tokenizer swallows: 22..24 where abcjs says
  22..25. `geometryOf` routes through `abcelemOf` now, so the join is stated once.

`tune.lines` is untouched by all of it — the projection reads only what was WRITTEN and
resolves its own copies, and it tells a pad from a written rest by `Rest.overlayPad` rather
than by a null range, which was true only while the padding was spanless and wrong.
