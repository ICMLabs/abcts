## 3. WHAT LANDED

### `renderAbc` renders ONE TUNE PER OUTPUT SLOT

`renderEngine` normalises a non-array `output` to `[output]`, opens at
`params.startingTune ?? 0`, and walks the SLOTS — a single div renders the FIRST tune and
nothing else, `"*"` is a headless slot, and a slot past the end of the book CLEARS its div
rather than being skipped (`api/abc_tunebook.js:56-104`).

Ours returned one object per TUNE and joined every `svg` into the one target. **No gate
could see it because every gate leaned on it**: 23 test and script files asked for
`renderAbc('paper', abc)[i]` and were handed an array our own implementation had
over-filled. They now call the API the way abcjs's own `dump-svg.js` does, through
`tests/render-all.ts`.

### The accessors, and the oracle that had to exist first

Nine numeric `AbcTune` accessors exported with abcjs's names — every one already here as a
private helper the clock is built on. `tests/corpus-accessors/golden.json` is **293 tunes
across both corpora, generated by RUNNING abcjs 6.7.0**; the table opened at 18 and closed
to 2. Six findings, none of which any existing gate could state:

1. **A LONE TEMPO WORD CARRIES A RATE AND DOES NOT PRINT IT.** `Q:"Adagio"` looks the word
   up in a 26-entry table and stamps `bpm: 68` with `suppressBpm: true`
   (`abc_parse_header.js:204-268`). `ave-verum-corpus` played at the 180 default while
   every SVG gate was green on it.
2. **A ZERO-LENGTH NOTE TAKES A QUARTER OF TIME.** `durationClassOveride` is
   `elem.duration * tripletmultiplier` and `AbsoluteElement` takes it only
   `if (options.durationClassOveride)` — **`0` IS FALSY** — so it falls through to the
   duration `createNote` already replaced with 0.25 under its own comment "zero duration
   will draw a quarter note head". `C0` draws as a stemless quarter, sounds for nothing,
   and occupies a quarter on the clock.
3. **`getMeter` IS THE STAFF'S METER**, which is the one stamped while the voice still held
   no note or bar — a STANDALONE `M:` replaces it, an INLINE `[M:]` only fills a gap, and a
   tune with no music has no lines at all and falls through to 4/4. Four fixtures pin every
   edge, and the fourth is what stopped the first attempt being wrong the other way.
4. **`getBpm` DEFAULTS TO THE HEADER'S TEMPO ALONE.** An inline `[Q:]` is a tempo ELEMENT
   and never reaches `metaText`.
5. **`getPickupLength` WALKS EVERY VOICE OF EVERY STAFF, ACCUMULATING**, and only a BAR
   returns — so a two-voice tune with no barline sums both voices.
6. **A MULTI-MEASURE REST TAKES ITS MEASURES.** Only the SPACING duration is flattened to
   1; `Z24 | F2 |` runs 32.667s and ours ran 2s.

### `\%` is a literal percent, and the note excluding it was STALE

abcjs 6.7.0 replaces `\%` with the sentinel `U+200B U+FF05` before anything else looks
(`abc_parse.js:511-512`) — two characters for two, so every source offset is unmoved, and
nothing restores it, so `T:100\% Amazing` really does draw a fullwidth percent.

`content-parity` carried a paragraph reasoning that the fixture came from 6.7.0 while our
target was 6.6.3 — **the target moved on 2026-08-08 and the note did not**, so a fixture
whose entire purpose is this rule sat excluded for a week with every note after the escape
silently dropped. The fifth time on this branch that a note naming a cause is the reason a
row stopped being read. `GOLDEN_FROM_ANOTHER_ABCJS` is now empty.

**And the ACCESSOR gate is what found it** — `escaped-percent` has a `.parse.json` golden
and no `.svg`, so no byte gate had ever opened it.

### The tune object, `TuneBook`, and the book helpers

Fifteen symbols wired onto what `renderAbc` returns (`version`, `media`, the eight
accessors, `setTiming`, `noteTimings`, the two totals, `setUpAudio`), plus `TuneBook`,
`numberOfTunes`, `parseOnly` and `signature`.

Two shapes are PROJECTIONS rather than our model, which is the ruling doing its work:
`getMeter` returns abcjs's `{type, value:[{num, den}]}` with the numbers as STRINGS, and a
compound numerator sums its parts. And `noteTimings` and the totals are **state, not
derived on read** — `getTotalTime()` before `setTiming()` is `undefined` from abcjs and is
`undefined` from us.

`TuneBook` keeps every quirk because a host slices files with it: the split is `"\nX:"` so
a tune must open a line, a tune ends at the first BLANK LINE rather than at the next `X:`,
file-wide `%%` directives are prepended to every tune, and the title is a split on `"T:"`
rather than a field lookup.

## 4. WHAT IS WRITTEN DOWN RATHER THAN BUILT

Three, each measured through both engines with abcjs's own algorithm cited, and each
gated so it cannot be forgotten:

- **A CHORD'S TIES ARE PER PITCH** (`tests/chord-tie.test.ts`, two `it.fails`).
  `[ceg]-|[ceg]` should be three notes of double length and we play six.
  `abc_midi_flattener.js:287-325` is per-PITCH duration with the tied head NULLED out and
  `element.duration` deleted; our `FlatItem` carries one duration and one `tiedOver` per
  EVENT, so porting it changes that shape inside a module byte-exact against three
  oracles. **The tests assert what abcjs does and go RED when the gap closes.**
- **`%%maxStaves` TRUNCATES THE CLOCK**, because `makeVoicesArray` walks the DRAWN
  staffgroups (`abc_tune.js:396-436`). `setTiming` runs over the parse tree on purpose, so
  this needs an architecture decision rather than a fix.
- **An `&` OVERLAY'S `end` ROW is a whole note late** while every EVENT row already
  matches — the overlay's padding, invisible through `noteTimings`.

## 4b. AND THEN `strTranspose` AND THE SYNTH TABLES — 44 → 32

**`strTranspose` is in at 58 of 59** (`src/str/transpose.ts`, `src/str/keys.ts`,
`tests/transpose.test.ts`). ABC text in, ABC text out: it does not re-print the tune, it
collects `{start, end, note}` replacements into the ORIGINAL string, sorts them backwards
so earlier indexes stay valid, and splices — so spacing, comments, decorations and lyrics
survive by construction.

**THE ORACLE IS WHAT abcjs DOES, NOT WHAT ITS SUITE ASSERTS**, and that earned itself on
the first run: `output-miss-accidental` expects `=B, _B,|_B, |` and abcjs produces
`=B, B,|_B, |` — the test is named for the bug it documents. The 59 inputs are harvested by
EVALUATING `transpose-output.test.js` with its helpers replaced; every expected string is
generated by RUNNING abcjs, with the test file's claim recorded beside it in
`testFileSays`.

Five findings, as the table fell from 50 differing to 1:

- **abcjs's ELEMENT SPAN opens at the first thing written for the note** — chord symbol,
  `!…!` or `+…+` decoration, grace group, and the space between. That is why `findNotes`
  masks the quoted and banged runs and does NOT mask the braces: a grace is transposed as
  an ordinary note of its element. Measured on Cooley's — `{c}B` is one element, 39…43.
- **A rest carries a chord symbol**, so `"Ab7b5"z` transposes.
- **A barline takes the pending chord**, and `el.chord` is tested before `el_type`.
- **A percussion staff is skipped**, and the clef may be the TUNE's (`K:clef=perc`).
- **Bagpipe music is never transposed** — `getKeySignature().root` really is `"Hp"`,
  measured, so the guard is live rather than dead.

One row measured and written down: `""_A-_A"D"|` puts a chord between the last note of a
measure and its barline and our parser drops it — `Measure.openingBarlineChord` covers an
OPENING barline only.

**And `abcjs.synth`'s silent half**, plus `test.Parse`/`test.EngraverController` (with
`renderTuneBook` delegating to it) and `renderEngine`. Both synth tables were MEASURED out
of abcjs's files and both are bigger than they look: `pitchToNoteName` runs 21 to **121**,
preferring flats the whole way, so its last entry is `Db9`; `instrumentIndexToName` has
**129** entries because abcjs appends `percussion` past the end of General MIDI.
