## 2. IT OPENED AT 675 OF 691, AND ONE FLAG WAS DOING TWO JOBS

The declared list — the mode table in `CLAUDE.md` — accounted for eleven. Measured, holding
everything else equal:

| source | fixtures |
|---|---|
| layout's `strict` flag | 331 |
| the emitter's `strict` flag | 333 |
| glyph table / line weights / spacing density / text metrics | 5 |
| the parser's `isStrict` — the only ones intended | 6 |

`strict` gated both *reproduce abcjs's bug* and *engrave the way abcjs engraves*, threaded
through two hundred sites without the distinction ever being drawn. So extended was not
"abcjs plus fixes" — it was **a second engraving engine that no gate compared to anything**:
Bravura outlines at Bravura's advances, abcm2ps's spacing density (16% looser), Bravura's
line weights, real per-em text metrics. Every one of those is a LOOK, not a bug abcjs has,
and not one was written down as a divergence.

`ABCJS_GAPS` in `layout.ts` is the half that survives — eight sites, each a row of the mode
table: the three-quarter-tone accidental, tremolo bars, the two `STRICT_UNDRAWN` decoration
passes, and the melisma `_` with its extender. 675 → 11.

**⭐ THE LESSON, AND IT IS THE SAME ONE THIS REPO KEEPS LEARNING.** The divergence list was
believed to be the whole list for weeks. Nothing was lying: every entry in it was true, and
the question *"what ELSE differs"* had simply never been asked in a form that could answer.
A gate is the only thing that can ask it. **A list of exceptions is not a measurement of
exceptions.**

## 3. ONE CORRECTION WAS MEASURED AND DECLINED — do not re-open

The golden text tables are ASCII-only: `dump-svg.js`'s `widths[ch] || 8` measures CJK, and
everything outside its five tables, at a flat 8px. So a Chinese lyric is SPACED narrower
than it is drawn, and extended measured for real instead. That is a genuine correction and
it looks like obvious work.

**It costs 156 of 691 fixtures to fix something no browser ever shows.** With a DOM both
modes ask it (`text-measure.ts`); the divergence existed only under jsdom. Both modes now
measure with the golden tables, and `realTextWidth` is unreachable in consequence — left in
place with the reasoning at the site.

## 4. AND A PIPELINE DIFFERENCE IS NOT A MODE DIFFERENCE

`scripts/zzsite.mjs`'s third column reported extended "way out of whack" for a day. Three of
its render options were not the ones compat passes:

- **`classes`** — panes 1 and 2 speak abcjs's vocabulary, pane 3 asked for core's, which
  takes a **different emitter path entirely**. That is what dropped the BOX round a
  `%%titlefont … box` row, and it was read as an extended-mode defect for an afternoon.
- **`staffSpace`** — `toSVG` defaults to 8px per staff space and compat passes abcjs's own
  7.75, so the pane came out 32/31 too wide (722.58 for a 700px page).
- **`staffwidth`** — **`core.render` has no such option.** The key is `systemWidth`, and it
  is the PAGE rather than the music area, so the value was silently dropped and the pane
  rendered at the engine default.

Held equal, all three panes measure 700 × 1006.31 on `visual-options-01-fonts`.

## 5. THE THREE THAT WERE LEFT — all closed the same day

1. ✅ **THE `classes: 'abcts'` PATH DREW NO BOX, IN EITHER MODE.** `%%titlefont … box` and
   `%%gchordfont … box` produced 2 boxes under `classes: "abcjs"` and 0 under `"abcts"`.
   **TWO SHAPES FOR ONE DIRECTIVE**, and fixing either alone leaves half the tune unboxed:
   a MUSIC text carries `box` + `boxSize` and measures its own rect (its emitter said
   `if (bs !== undefined && abcjs)`), while a BLOCK row carries a placed `boxRect` drawn by
   the abcjs top-text pass alone, which the core path has no equivalent of. The text's own
   `boxDx`/`baselineY` shifts were abcjs-only too. `tests/classes-vocabulary.test.ts`, with
   a negative control on the same tune — a counter that counts anything passes the positive
   row alone.

2. ✅ **`AbcjsParams.mode`** — abcts's one addition to abcjs's params, defaulting to strict.
   **FOUR hard-wired literals**, not one: the parse, the layout, the emitter (which had no
   `mode` at all and leaned on `defaultMode`) and `getMeasureWidths`, which lays the tune
   out AGAIN at width 0. `renderInto` resolves it once and hands it to all four.
   `tests/compat-mode.test.ts` has a row per site.

   ⚠️ **TWO OF ITS ROWS WERE MUTE AS FIRST WRITTEN.** The measure-width control: a single
   `^3/2C` reports 48.24 in BOTH modes, because one accidental is absorbed by the room the
   note already takes at a minimum layout — six shapes were measured before one moved. And
   the compat-vs-core comparison matched on `transform="translate(…)"` and found **zero**
   paths in both engines, since abcjs bakes a glyph's position into the first `M` of its
   path data; it would have compared two empty lists and passed. A count assertion caught
   it. **That is four mute probes in two days on this branch** — write the control that
   fails first.

3. ✅ `Docs/ABCJS-DIFFERENCES.md`'s blockers section described the SVG byte arc as open. It
   is marked history now, and kept: each row is a shape that recurs.
