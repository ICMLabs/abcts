## WHERE TO START

`CHECKPOINT-2026-08-05.md` has the closed table and the five that are left, each with what
to measure. `tests/line-weights.test.ts` is where a new case goes — add the fixture and the

The slur/tie four are the biggest and the least mechanical: abcjs builds its own path in
`draw/tie.js` rather than stroking a constant width, so porting them is a shape port and
should probably wait behind the raw-`GLYPHS` audit, which is cheaper and likelier to move
noteheads.

## WHAT NOT TO CHASE

- **An arithmetic order-of-operations difference.** Measured and ruled out — finding 69.
- **The staff line's residual 0.002px.** That is abcjs's own `roundNumber` quantisation;
  our value is the exact one — finding 70.
- **Arithmetic on a one-notehead fixture.** No spread, so its `dy`/`dx` of 0.00 mean nothing.
