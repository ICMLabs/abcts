## WHAT CLOSED, AND THE RULE EACH ONE PAID FOR

### 1. `deline` — 1,566 → 1,610, CLOSED

**THE KEY BEFORE THE FIRST `K:` IS `none`, NOT C MAJOR** (`abc_parse.js:80`), and an
unparseable `K:` never writes it — `getKeyPitch` finds nothing in `cm`, a bare `K:` or a
`K: style=harmonic`. The two print identically and only `root` separates them.

**A DECORATION DOES NOT REACH BACK ACROSS AN UNREADABLE CHARACTER.** `startI` is retaken by
every junk character, while `el` — decorations, chord symbol, grace group — SURVIVES an
iteration that appended nothing.

**AND `objEqual`'s REPLACER IS NOT AN IGNORE — `abselem`'s PRESENCE STILL COUNTS.** Only a
DRAWN line carries one (`draw/absolute.js:72`), so `%%maxStaves` makes `deline` unshift a
clef in front of every hidden line. A key with nothing to draw has no element either
(`create-key-signature.js:8-9`), and neither does `clef=none`.

### 2. `Editor` — 194 → 196, CLOSED

**A CLEF CARRIES ITS OFFSET.** `placeElement` reads `g.dx` when it has one and falls back to
`g.x - el.x`, which subtracts the element's own x back out and lands one ULP away.

### 3. `tuneMetrics` — 215 → 218, CLOSED

**A VOICE SWITCH THROWS A PENDING CANCELLATION AWAY.** `setCurrentVoice` restores the key
with `deepCopyKey`, which does NOT copy `impliedNaturals` (`abc_parse_key_voice.js:535-537`).
That is the whole difference between `ragtime-nightingale` (written voice by voice, so its
`[K:Eb]` reaches the next line) and `inline-key-per-voice` (alternating `V:1`/`V:2`, so its
naturals die at the switch). **The rule the previous session could not find was a property of
how the tune is LAID OUT IN SOURCE, not of what it writes.**

**AND abcjs DERIVES THE SHIFT IT HANDS THE OTHER VOICES.** `layoutOneItem` moves the cursor
with `x += extraWidth - er` and RETURNS it; the caller takes `var dx = voicechildx - x`
(`layout/staff-group.js:85-91`). `21.775 - 7.333819891893739` is `14.44118010810626`; read
back off the cursor it is `14.441180108106238`.

### 4. `setGlyph` — 5 → 6, CLOSED

**A DISPLACED GLYPH IS RE-PLACED, NOT NUDGED.** The voice-overlap rule rewrites `child.dx`
alone and lets `setX` spend it as one add onto the element's x.

### 5. `parseOnly` — 308 → 1,570 of 1,570, CLOSED

abcjs's `parseOnly` is `renderEngine` with a callback that does nothing
(`api/abc_tunebook.js:42-54`): PARSED and never LAID OUT. Ours was `renderAbc(['*'])`.

- **NOTHING IS DRAWN WHEN NOTHING ENGRAVED.** The eager `toSVG` is skipped, and
  `selectablesOf` is what stamps `highestVert`, `averagepitch`, `minpitch`, `maxpitch` and
  `printer_shift` as it walks what was DRAWN. 1,100 rows, and `parseOnly` lays nothing out at
  all now.
- ⚠️ **THE ENGRAVER RENAMES THE ELEMENT IT DRAWS.** An unengraved tune says `key` and
  `meter`, and the STAFF's furniture carries no `el_type` at all. ⚠️ **EVERY TEST ON AN
  ELEMENT'S KIND NEEDS BOTH SPELLINGS** — `FIELD_ELEMENTS`, the hoist's `STAFF_FIELD` and
  `noWarnBeforeTitle` each silently stopped firing.
- **AND THEN THE GATE WAS WIDENED TO EVERY STAFF FIELD** — 1,451 rows became 1,520 — naming
  `brace`/`bracket`/`connectBarLines` (fifty tunes), the FONT OBJECT SHAPE (ours was the
  model's `{face, size, bold, italic}`), `%%barnumbers 0`'s staff number, and `el.fonts`.
- Smaller: a clef's `stafflines` and `clef=none`'s absent `clefPos`, a tempo's `type` (the
  ENGRAVER's, under abcjs's own TODO), `!class=…!` and `!style=x!`.

⚠️ **AND THE `sequence` GATE WAS READING A PARSED TUNE AGAINST A RENDERED ORACLE.** Its

**A gate that agrees for the wrong reason agrees until something unrelated moves.**

### 6. THE UNGATED BEHAVIOURS ARE GATED — and both were already right

**`CreateSynth`'s error arms**, six cases: `init` NEVER reports a load failure
(`_loadBatch`'s trailing `.catch` swallows it, so a 404 soundfont resolves `init` looking
clean and `prime` throws); **the failure is CACHED**, so a second `init` requests nothing and
reports the dead notes as `cached`; a pitch outside `pitchToNoteName` is labelled
`"…:undefined"` and still primes to a silent buffer; `duration <= 0` resolves
`{status: "empty", seconds: 0}`.

⚠️ **THE FOURTH ARM THE HANDOFF NAMED CANNOT BE DRIVEN AT ALL.** `status: "pending"` is
produced NOWHERE in abcjs 6.7.0 — `load-note.js` resolves `"loaded"` or rejects — so the
retry with the doubling delay, its 90-second ceiling and its recursive `_loadBatch` are dead
code in the reference, and ours ports them faithfully dead.

**`runWhenReady`'s `isLoading` arm**: ⚠️ `setTune(tune, false, …)` NEVER sets `isLoading` —
the passive arm returns without calling `go()` — so `userAction = true` is the case. The gate
stubs `setTimeout` to a no-op, so the arm was unreachable by construction; the new case
installs a RECORDING timer whose delays are part of the comparison. ⚠️ `TimingCallbacks`'s
backup `joggerTimer` reschedules ITSELF, so a timer that always fires never stops.

### 7. THE THREE SLUR PLACEMENTS THE MODEL COULD NOT SAY

`Pitch` carries `slurStarts`/`slurEnds` — on the pitch OBJECT, so the projection's
sort-by-pitch carries the mark with its head — `GracePitch` inherits both instead of owning a
partial copy, and `Rest` gains `slurEnds`.

⚠️ **A `)` CLOSES ON A REST AND A `(` DOES NOT OPEN ON ONE.** One line: the rest arm assigns
both (`abc_parse_music.js:516-518`) and `delete el.startSlur` runs unconditionally four lines
later (`:527`), where nothing deletes `endSlur`.

The numbering itself is a line-by-line port of `cleanUpSlursInLine`: **one open stack PER
CHORD POSITION** — 0 on the element, `p + 1` on a head, 20 on a grace, so the labels are 1,
101, 201, 2001 — a close that finds its own position empty takes ANY other, and with nothing
open anywhere it INVENTS `chordPos * 100 + 1`.

**`abcts-slur-shapes.abc` gates all nine shapes**, because neither corpus wrote one, and it
found what the projection cannot see:

⚠️ **abcjs's OWN NUMBERING REFUSES TO PAIR TWO OF THEM, AND IT DRAWS EACH END AS A HALF.** A
slur inside a chord opens 101 on pitch 0 and closes 201 on pitch 1 — different labels, so
`slurs[slurid]` finds neither — and a rest never closes one because `addSlursAndTies` has
only two call sites and a rest has no `pitches`. **So the model says closed and the drawing
says open, on purpose**, and both are stated where they are read. Pairing them in the drawing
looked right and drew a curve abcjs does not.

⚠️ **AND A GRACE IS ALWAYS STEM-UP TO THE DIRECTION RULE** — abcjs passes the literal `"up"`
where every other call site passes the note's own (`abstract-engraver.js:498`).
