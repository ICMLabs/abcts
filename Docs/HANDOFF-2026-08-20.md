## WHAT CLOSED, AND THE RULE EACH ONE PAID FOR

### 1. `deline` — 1,566 → 1,570

**THE KEY BEFORE THE FIRST `K:` IS `none`, NOT C MAJOR.** `multilineVars.key` opens at
`{root: 'none', …}` (`abc_parse.js:80`) and an unparseable `K:` never writes it — `getKeyPitch`
finds nothing in `cm`, in a bare `K:` or in `K: style=harmonic`, so the key in force simply
stays. The two print identically and only `root` separates them. `K:none` itself reported
`root: "C"` on every tune that writes one.

**AND A DECORATION DOES NOT REACH BACK ACROSS AN UNREADABLE CHARACTER.** `startI` is retaken
by every junk character, while `el` — the decorations, the chord symbol, the grace group —
SURVIVES an iteration that appended nothing. `frere-jacques`'s own `U:t = !tenuto!` makes its
`S:` prose parse that way.

**AND `objEqual`'s REPLACER IS NOT AN IGNORE — `abselem`'s PRESENCE STILL COUNTS.** Only a
line that was DRAWN carries one (`draw/absolute.js:72`), so `%%maxStaves` makes `deline`
unshift a clef in front of every hidden line. `tune.deline` takes `Layout.systems.length` now.
Two of the three fields need more than the line's drawn-ness: **a key with nothing to draw has
no element** (`create-key-signature.js:8-9`) and **`clef=none` has none** (`create-clef.js:26`).

### 2. `Editor` — 194 → 196

**A CLEF CARRIES ITS OFFSET.** `placeElement` reads `g.dx` when it has one and falls back to
`g.x - el.x`, which subtracts the element's own x back out and lands one ULP away. The clef
indent and the octave marker's `dx + adjustspacing` are declared now.

### 3. `tuneMetrics` — 215 → 217

**A VOICE SWITCH THROWS A PENDING CANCELLATION AWAY.** `setCurrentVoice` restores the key with
`deepCopyKey`, which copies `accidentals`/`root`/`acc`/`mode`/`explicitAccidentals` and NOT
`impliedNaturals` (`abc_parse_key_voice.js:167-179`, `:535-537`). So the naturals survive only
to the next line of the SAME voice. That is the whole difference between `ragtime-nightingale`
(written voice by voice — its `[K:Eb]` reaches the next line) and `inline-key-per-voice`
(alternating `V:1`/`V:2` — its `[K:F]` naturals die at the switch). **The rule the previous
session could not find was a property of how the tune is LAID OUT IN SOURCE, not of what it
writes.**

**AND abcjs DERIVES THE SHIFT IT HANDS THE OTHER VOICES.** `layoutOneItem` moves the cursor
with `x += extraWidth - er` and RETURNS it; the caller takes `var dx = voicechildx - x` and
gives THAT to `shiftRight` (`layout/staff-group.js:85-91`). `21.775 - 7.333819891893739` is
`14.44118010810626`; the same shift read back off the cursor is `14.441180108106238`.

### 4. `setGlyph` — 5 → 6

**A DISPLACED GLYPH IS RE-PLACED, NOT NUDGED.** The voice-overlap rule rewrites `child.dx`
alone (`layout/voice-elements.js:56-62`) and lets `setX` spend it as one add onto the
element's x. Ours moved the glyph AND the offset, making it `(el.x + off) + width`.

## 6. THE UNGATED BEHAVIOURS ARE GATED

Both symbols `HANDOFF-2026-08-19.md` §3 listed are driven now, and the port answered every
step correctly on the first run.

**`CreateSynth`'s error arms** — six cases, and abcjs's answers are worth knowing:
`init` NEVER reports a load failure (`_loadBatch`'s trailing `.catch` swallows it, so a
soundfont that 404s resolves `init` looking clean and `prime` throws); **the failure is
CACHED**, so a second `init` requests nothing and reports the dead notes as `cached`; a
pitch outside `pitchToNoteName` is labelled `"…:undefined"` and still primes to a silent
buffer; and `duration <= 0` resolves `{status: "empty", seconds: 0}`.

⚠️ **AND THE FOURTH ARM THE HANDOFF NAMED CANNOT BE DRIVEN AT ALL.** `status: "pending"` is
produced NOWHERE in abcjs 6.7.0 — `load-note.js` resolves `"loaded"` or rejects — so the
retry with the doubling delay, its 90-second ceiling and its recursive `_loadBatch` are
**dead code in the reference**, and ours ports them faithfully dead.

**`runWhenReady`'s `isLoading` arm** — ⚠️ `setTune(tune, false, …)` NEVER sets `isLoading`
(the passive arm returns without calling `go()`), so a `play()` racing it takes the arm the
other cases already cover; `userAction = true` is the case. The gate stubs `setTimeout` to a
no-op, so the arm was unreachable by construction; the new case installs a RECORDING timer
whose delays are part of the comparison. ⚠️ `TimingCallbacks`'s backup `joggerTimer`

## 7. THE THREE SLUR PLACEMENTS THE MODEL COULD NOT SAY — AND A FIXTURE FOR THEM

The two `ponytail:` notes the slur port left, plus the grace half beside them, are closed in
the MODEL. `Pitch` carries `slurStarts`/`slurEnds` — on the pitch OBJECT, so the
projection's sort-by-pitch carries the mark with its head — `GracePitch` inherits both
instead of owning `slurEnds`, and `Rest` gains `slurEnds` alone.

⚠️ **A `)` CLOSES ON A REST AND A `(` DOES NOT OPEN ON ONE.** One line: the rest arm assigns
both (`abc_parse_music.js:516-518`) and `delete el.startSlur` runs unconditionally four
lines later (`:527`), where nothing deletes `endSlur`.

**AND `abcts-slur-shapes.abc` GATES ALL NINE SHAPES**, because neither corpus wrote one. It
found a rendering gap nothing else could:

⚠️ **abcjs's OWN NUMBERING REFUSES TO PAIR TWO OF THEM, AND IT DRAWS EACH END AS A HALF.**
A slur inside a chord opens 101 on pitch 0 and closes 201 on pitch 1 — different labels, so
`slurs[slurid]` finds neither — and a rest never closes one because `addSlursAndTies` has
only two call sites and a rest has no `pitches`. **So the model says closed and the drawing
says open, on purpose.** `calcX` has an arm for each missing anchor and `draw/tie.js:39-40`
forces `isTie = true` for either.

⚠️ **AND A GRACE IS ALWAYS STEM-UP TO THE DIRECTION RULE** — abcjs passes the literal `"up"`
where every other call site passes the note's own (`abstract-engraver.js:498`).
