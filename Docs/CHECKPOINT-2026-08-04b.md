## FINDINGS 41–50

### 41. `calcWidth` — the goldens' own text metrics, and they ARE the target

`dump-svg.js:62-84` picks ONE of five ASCII per-character tables by font SIZE ALONE — not by
family — sums `widths[ch] || 8`, and takes the widest line. Three of the six size brackets
ask for a key that does not exist in `dump-elements-char-widths.js` (`titlefont`,
`subtitlefont`, and the bold arm below 17px), so they fall through to `repeatfont`: **a 27px
title is measured with 17px Times widths.** `"Simple C"` is 63.81px, and that is what the
golden centres.

Ported into `src/renderer/golden-widths.ts` (generated by `scripts/gen-golden-widths.mjs`)
and `goldenTextWidth` in `layout.ts`. **Indexed by UTF-16 code UNIT**, as the generator's
`lines[li][i]` is — an astral character measures 8 twice, not once.

`visual-tablature-17` **499.38 → 40.84**; `little swallow`'s dx 24.19 → 21.69.

**The mode split is one module-level switch**, `STRICT_TEXT_METRICS`, set from the mode in
`layoutScore`. `abcjs-extended` keep the real per-em tables in `text-metrics.ts`.
Threading a `strict` argument through `textWidth`'s sixteen call sites was the alternative
and most of them do not have the mode in scope.

### 42. `%%jazzchords`, and a nested tspan is a whole LINE of height

`translateChord` (`translate-chord.js:12-34`) splits a chord into root, modifier and `/bass`
and rejoins them round a `\x03` marker; `svg.js:198-211` reads that marker and nests a
`font-size:0.7em` tspan per part. The bass drops `0.4em` clear of a raised modifier and
`0.1em` from the baseline without one.

**The geometry is the point.** `getTextSize` measures the very markup `svg.js` will draw, and
the generator counts nested tspans as separate LINES — `h + (n-1) * fontSize * 1.2`
(`dump-svg.js:120-124`). So `"x/C"` comes back three lines high and the chord lane reserves
**38.4px** more. `visual-misc-03` 38.37 → 0.07.

### 43. A DECLARED BOX IS THE PUBLISHED `h`, NOT THE DERIVED INK BOX — the systemic 0.03px

`symbolHeightInPitches` reads `glyphs[symbol].h` (`glyphs.js:161-164`). The G clef publishes
**57.057px** against an ink box of **57.09**, and a sharp publishes 20.15 against 20.19 —
0.033 and 0.04px, a fiftieth of a staff space each.

That was the systemic `oy` the previous checkpoint recorded as measured and undiagnosed:
every fixture carried ~0.03 and a sharp key signature added ~0.04, on `K:C` / `K:A` control
pairs with nothing else in them. **The clef sets the staff top on a plain tune, so it reached
everything.** Three reserve sites — the clef's box, a time-signature digit's `thickness`, and
a key-signature accidental's — all meant `symbolHeightInPitches` and all three read the ink
box. **Eleven fixtures came off the ranked table on it.**

`ResolvedGlyph` has carried both numbers (`height` vs `declaredHeight`) since the table was
built. The bug was reading the wrong field, not a missing one.

### 44. AN ENDING LANE OVER A CHORD LANE COSTS A FLAT 2 PITCH

```js
if (staff.specialY.endingHeightAbove) {
  if (staff.specialY.chordHeightAbove) staff.top += 2;
  else staff.top += staff.specialY.endingHeightAbove + margin;
```

(`set-upper-and-lower-elements.js:33-38`). **A different BRANCH, not a scaling** — 2 pitch
against a volta's 5 + 1 is 15.5px. A TUPLET takes the same branch, since abcjs keeps both in
the one `endingHeightAbove`. Found by a ladder of five: it appears on `"D7"…|1…` and on
nothing simpler.

### 45. A CHORD'S LANE IS ITS MEASURED HEIGHT, not a ratio of the default

Three terms, all the generator's: its size table with `size + 2` for anything unlisted (so
`%%gchordfont Arial 80` resolves to 107px and reserves **109**, where a ratio gives 124); one
whole LINE per nested tspan; and `padding * 4` when the font is BOXED
(`get-text-size.js:46-48`). `visual-tablature-17` boxes five sizes and its dy went
**96.2 → 0.0**.

### 46. A TUPLET'S BRACKET IS INK THE CHORD LANE SITS ON

`layoutVoice` calls `voice.adjustRange` on every `TripletElem` (`layout/voice.js:19-23`)
BEFORE `setUpperAndLowerElements` runs. `anchorAboveStaff`'s ink call was leaving
`tupletReserves` out, so the chord lane landed UNDER the bracket and the outer pass took the
bracket instead — **0.779 pitch, which is `chordHeightAbove` minus the tuplet lane**, on
every staff carrying both.

### 47. AN ANNOTATION'S PLACEMENT DECIDES ITS RESERVE, and only two of five take a lane

`RelativeElement`'s `case "text"` gives `chordHeightAbove` **only when the pitch is
UNDEFINED** (`relative-element.js:68-75`). `above` and `below` pass none and take the lane;
`left`, `right` and `@x,y` are pitched on the note and take none.

```
left   roomTaken += width + 7;  x = -roomTaken;      addExtra -> extraw
right  roomTakenRight += 4;     x = roomTakenRight;  addRight -> w = dx + width + 4
@x,y   w: 0, pitch = minpitch + (y + 3 * STEP) / STEP, and nothing else
```

(`add-chord.js:50-96`). A bare `"<F"F` put the note **16.78px** left of abcjs's, which is
`F`'s 9.78 in the annotation font plus the 7. And `@` went into the chord lane with its
coordinates printed — `"@1,1E"e` alone reserved 22.38px abcjs does not.

**A pitched annotation reserves a POINT, not its letters**: `RelativeElement`'s `top` and
`bottom` are both the pitch unless a `thickness` says otherwise, and `add-chord` passes none.

### 48. TWO CHORD SYMBOLS ON ONE NOTE STACK — they do not replace each other

`el.chord[ci].name += "\n" + chordName` for the same position
(`abc_parse_music.js:200-205`), and `chordString` makes each LINE its own centred mark,
walking `j` DOWN from the last (`add-chord.js:39-41`). So `"D""G"d` opens a second chord
LANE — 18.52px of staff, and all of `visual-transpose-04`'s residual. A `;` inside one name
means the same thing. Our `chordSymbol` field was overwriting, which also LOST the first
name.

### 49. A REST CARRIES ITS CHORD SYMBOL AND ANNOTATIONS

abcjs runs `addChord` over every abselem it builds regardless of type
(`abstract-engraver.js:853`), so `"Eb7"z` prints the chord over the rest and reserves the
whole chord lane — **22.4px** on a tune that opens that way. Our model gave a `Rest`
decorations and nothing else, so the mark was **lost**, not mispositioned.

### 50. AN ANNOTATION IS LEFT-JUSTIFIED AT ITS ELEMENT; A CHORD SYMBOL IS CENTRED ON IT

`getChordDim`'s `offset = this.type === "chord" ? this.realWidth / 2 : 0`
(`relative-element.js:96`), and an annotation's `RelativeElement` has `dx` 0. **The golden
says the same thing in one attribute**: `text-anchor="start"` on an annotation beside
`text-anchor="middle"` on a chord. Centring ours reached further left than abcjs's and opened
a second chord LANE — 18.51px on `"Ab"z"^break"c2`.

### 50b. AN ADDITIVE METER IS DRAWN TERM BY TERM

abcjs keeps the numerator as the string it was written as — the golden's own
`data-name="2+3"` — and lays out one glyph per character with the `+` between terms, each
`getSymbolWidth("+") + 2` wide (`create-time-signature.js:17-27`). Summing to `5` cost
`M:2+3/8` **17.08px** of prefix and moved every note on the line. `Meter.numeratorParts`
carries the terms; `numerator` stays the sum, since that is the bar's duration either way.
The `+` glyph is authored in `glyphs.ts` — two rectangles, no font licence.

**Its denominator loop is a real abcjs bug and is reproduced by construction**: `for (i2 = 0;
i2 < elem.value[i].num.length; i2++) denWidth += getSymbolWidth(elem.value[i].den[i2])`
iterates the NUMERATOR's length while indexing the DENOMINATOR.
