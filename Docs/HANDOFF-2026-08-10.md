### 1. THE TITLE'S CENTRE — a real defect, and my attempt is written up so you do not repeat it

abcjs centres the title on the **PAPER** (`x="350"` on a 700px page); we emit **123.0809**.
The rule is already in `ScoreMetadata`'s own comment: *"`%%center` centres on the STAFF
width — 335, not the paper's 350 the title uses."* **No pixel gate compares text POSITION**,
so it has been wrong on every titled tune.

`CHECKPOINT-2026-08-10.md` §4 has the measurement. The short version: `topTextBlock` is
called once, at `layout.ts:7493`, with `systemWidth - marginX * 2`, and centres on
`width / 2`. Changing that argument to `systemWidth + marginX * 2` — which the arithmetic
says gives exactly 350px — **changed nothing**, so the width arriving there is not what the
call site suggests. `15.8814 * 2 = 31.7628` staff spaces is what it actually centred on.
**Print `width` inside `topTextBlock` first.** Do not reason about it from the call site;
that is what cost the attempt.
