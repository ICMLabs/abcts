## WHAT CLOSED, AND THE RULE EACH ONE PAID FOR

### 1. THE DECORATION TABLES — 96 NAMES, 93 COMPARABLE, THREE WRONG

`abc_parse_settings.js` holds FIVE lists, not one, and this parser already accepted all 96
names. What it had never done is DRAW each of them and compare.

- **`!slide!`'s EXTENT.** Its curve was already byte-exact and its reserve was missing
  entirely, so only the root `height` showed it — 154.552 against 139.052.
  ⚠️ **AND THE EXTENT BOWS THE OTHER WAY FROM THE DRAWING.** `getYBounds` branches on
  `isTie`, which nothing sets at layout time, so it takes `calcSlurDirection`, where a
  slide's undefined `voiceNumber` falls through to a `stemDir` read off two BLANKS that
  have none: `above` is FALSE, unconditionally and for every tune. The DRAWING recomputes
  `isTie` and reaches `calcTieDirection`, which says above. Reading the drawing's direction
  paid back exactly ONE of the four pitch.
- **`~(` / `~)` ARE `glissando(` / `glissando)`** — case labels sharing an arm in the
  WRITER's switch (`decoration.js:341-348`), not pseudonyms the parser rewrites, so nothing
  upstream ever spells them. Ours drew no squiggle at all.
- **A GLISSANDO INSETS BY HALF THE ELEMENT, NOT HALF THE NOTEHEAD.** `drawGlissando` reads
  `anchor1.w` off the ABSOLUTE element, which an unbeamed up-stem flag grows past the head
  — instrumented, 15.902 against `head.w` 9.81. `NoteAnchor.absWidth` now carries abcjs's
  `max(dx + w)` over the `addRight` children ONLY: heads, unbeamed flag, dots. An
  accidental is `addExtra`, a ledger `addFixed`, a decoration `addFixedX`; none touches `w`.

⚠️ **`!glissando)!` AND `!~)!` WITH NOTHING OPEN CRASH abcjs** — `drawGlissando` logs
"Glissando Element not set" and then reads `params.anchor1.heads[0]` anyway
(`draw/glissando.js:6-10`). No golden can exist for either. Ours renders both, and that
asymmetry is deliberate: a crash is not parity.

### 2. `checkLastBarX` — FOUND BY A CONTROL, NOT BY A VARIABLE

The V: probe reported **0 of 96 identical**, which is not a result about modifiers at all.
The two-voice control itself diverged, and the cause is the one place abcjs departs from
the pure time cursor this engine reproduces: `checkLastBarX`, at the end of
`layoutStaffGroup` (`layout/staff-group.js:3-20, 119`). Interior bars fall where each
voice's own time puts them; the FINAL one is pulled right, or a voice that runs out of
music early leaves its closing rule hanging mid-staff.

⚠️ **IT IS A ONE-DIRECTIONAL FORWARD PASS, WHICH IS abcjs's OWN BUG AND IS KEPT.** `maxX`
accumulates as it walks, so a voice wider than everything BEFORE it sets the new max and is
never moved. `abcts-last-bar-tune1` gates exactly that — three voices with the longest in
the middle, so the first keeps its short bar and only the third is pushed.

⚠️ **AND ONLY THE RULE MOVES, NOT THE ELEMENT.** abcjs assigns `maxChild.children[0].x`.
Moving the whole element is byte-identical on the drawing and WRONG on the element x every
host reads back — three ratcheted `makeVoicesArray` rows caught it, and the byte gate could
not have. **A SECOND GATE IS WHAT SAID THE FIRST ONE'S GREEN WAS NOT ENOUGH.**

### 3. THE V: MODIFIERS — THREE MORE

- **`^8` AND `_8` ARE CLEF OCTAVE SUFFIXES.** `parseKeyVoice` appends whichever of
  `- + ^ _` precedes an `8` to the clef name verbatim (`:511`) and the switch has a case
  for each of the eight spellings. ⚠️ `+16`/`-16` are NOT suffixes — the rule tests for an
  `8` — so `bass^16` is a plain bass in both engines and always was.
- **`cl=` IS `clef=`**, one case label sharing its arm (`:641`). V:-only; we read it on K:
  too, which only a `K:C cl=bass` could show.
- **THE STAFF-CONNECTING RULE STARTS AT THE FIRST STAFF'S OWN TOP LINE.** `calcY(10)` is
  only the fallback assigned before `printStaff` runs; `drawStaffGroup:143` reads back
  `staffs[0].topLine`, which is `calcY(numLines * linePitch)` — its highest DRAWN rule. A
  `stafflines=3` upper staff hung 15.5px of rule above a staff that is not there.
  ⚠️ **A ONE-LINE STAFF IS THE EXCEPTION** and keeps the full pitch-10 span, which
  `printStaff` returns regardless of where it drew (`draw/staff.js:14-17`).
