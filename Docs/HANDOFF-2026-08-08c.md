### 1. REPEATS — 6 cases, and the algorithm is already written out

`no-start-repeat-part`, `-title`, `-repeat`, `repeat-3`, `flatten-repeat`,
`flatten-regular-tie`, `flatten-break2`. abcjs unrolls them in the SEQUENCER with a
`Repeats` object (`synth/repeats.js`) that turns `|:`, `:|`, `|1`, `|2` into a linear
stream. Both of its passes are set out in `CHECKPOINT-2026-08-08c.md` — record only the
interesting bars, then fold them into `{common, endings[]}` spans and emit. The sparse
`endings` array indexed by ENDING NUMBER is what makes `|1,3` and `|2,4` interleave.

Our AST has `Measure.volta`, `openingBarline` and `closingBarline`; nothing reads them for
audio. The natural shape is a pass over `sequenceVoice`'s output, since that is already a
flat list with `bar` rows in it.

### 2. THE MID-TUNE `%%MIDI` — parsed, and the flattener ignores it

`Measure.midiCommands` carries `gchord`, `gchordon/off`, `bassprog`, `chordprog`,
`bassvol`, `chordvol`, `drum`. The chord track already has a setter for each
(`setGChord`, `setBassProg`, `setChordProg`, `gChordOn`); nothing calls them.
`flatten-change-gchord` and `cancel-gchord` are the cases.

### 3. `&` OVERLAY VOICES — 4 cases

`flatten-overlay`, `flatten-pickup-overlay`, `overlay-repeat`, `flatten-rep-and-over`.
`Measure.overlays` is already parsed and already rendered; the sequencer has to append each
as its own voice.

### 4. THE SMALL ONES, each a single case

- **A quarter tone is a PITCH BEND.** `{pitch: 71, cents: -50}`, not `pitch: 70.5`.
  `Note.microtoneCents` is already parsed.
- **Grace notes** — `style: "grace"`, half the main note's duration, `velocity * 2/3`, and
  the main note is shortened and pushed later by its own halved duration.
- **Note modifications** — `!trill!`, `!mordent!`, `!turn!`, `!roll!` become RUNS of 1/32
  notes with `style: "decoration"` (`doModifiedNotes`).
- **Per-note dynamics inside a chord** — `volumesPerNotePitch`, the `pitchIndexOfNote`
  argument to `processVolume`. `volume-in-chords` is the only case.

### 5. AFTER THE FLATTENER: the two surfaces deliberately left out

`timing.test.js`'s `setTiming` (event list joined to rendered geometry) and
`midi.test.js`'s MIDI FILE writer (`abc_midi_renderer.js`, `get-midi-file.js`). Both have
exact oracles sitting in abcjs's tests; neither is worth harvesting until the flattener
passes.
