## WHAT IS LEFT

### 1. ONE BYTE ROW, AND THE DIRECTIVE IS NOT THE CAUSE

`abcts-directives-tune4` — `%%stafftopmargin 30` on a tune WITH a title — differs in the
last bits of the root `height` (`201.76700000000002` against `201.767`) and in nothing else:
every staff line, every glyph, every text is identical.

**IT IS NOT THE MARGIN.** The same tune without a title is byte-identical in both engines
(138.492 / 76.64), and TWO different formulations of the margin — `raise(px / STEP)` and
`top -= px` with the pitch added separately — produce the identical double. What is left is
the y-versus-pitch asymmetry a staff carrying a HEADING BLOCK already has, recorded at
`verticalExtent` since 2026-08-11: `calcHeight` sums `staff.top` in PITCH and multiplies
once, while a block's span has no pitch and keeps the y form. **That is the deferred
refactor, not a new defect** — see the note at the staff-placement site, which also records
the shape that was tried and made it worse.

## WHAT CLOSED, AND THE RULE EACH ONE PAID FOR

### 1. THE OVERLAY PAD — IDENTITY *AND* VALUE

⚠️ **A JOIN NOBODY HAD MADE, WHOSE HOOK WAS ALREADY THERE.** `core/overlays.ts` runs on BOTH
sides — the parser back-fills the MODEL, the projection back-fills `tune.lines` — and
carries an opaque `ref` its back-fill copies onto the pad as `pad`. Tagging each PROJECTED
element with its own event before the pass makes every pad name the event it MIRRORS, which
is the event the model's pad names through `overlayMirrors`. `abcelemOf`'s synthesized third
object is dead for it now.

⚠️ **THE TAGS COME OFF BY WALKING THE LINES, NOT `byEvent`** — the ending-mark sweep
shallow-COPIES the last voice's elements, so the published object is not always the one
`byEvent` holds. That same copy is why a LAYER'S OWN NOTES were unstamped too.

⚠️ **AND `bd67ae6`'s VALUE FIX WAS RIGHT FOR ONE LINE AND WRONG FOR THE OTHER.** Its note
said abcjs's rendered `flattener-21` has NO `stem` element in any voice; dumping that tune's
`tune.lines` denies it — `L3/s0/v1` reads `STEM:down r bar n bar`. **A SOURCE READ THAT
PREDICTS AN ABSENCE IS STILL A PREDICTION: DUMP THE OBJECT.**

### 2. `abcLine.nonMusic` — A WHOLE FIELD THAT WAS NOT BUILT

`constructTuneElements` hangs a `Subtitle`, a `FreeText` or a `Separator` on every nonMusic
LINE at engrave time and a host reads `line.nonMusic`. The rows are the LAYOUT's own moves
and texts, joined back by block IDENTITY (`Layout.nonMusicRows`, keyed by `FreeTextBlock`) —
nothing re-derived, no `Layout` retained. Four rules came out of it: a nonMusic row is NOT
`addTextIf`'s shape and its class is UNCONDITIONAL; an absent span is ABSENT, not `-2`; the
row's text is the WHOLE BLOCK and is the same string its SELECTABLE carries; and a subtitle
above the first non-subtitle line gets nothing.

### 3. THE `ponytail:` SWEEP — FOUR PASSES, THIRTEEN DEFECTS

Fixtures `abcts-ledger-gaps.abc`, `-2`, `-3`, `-4` — twenty-eight tunes, every one a shape
an entry NAMED and then declared unwritten by either corpus. The rules worth carrying:

- **`%%scale` WAS NOT MODELLED**, and ⚠️ **PRINT AND THE SCALE WERE ONE FLAG** — abcjs gates
  `spacing.top` and the eleven-inch floor on `isPrint` where the transform keys on the
  SCALE, so a screen `%%scale` took print's lead and came out 1320px tall.
- **AN UNPARSEABLE TOKEN ENDS A CHORD WHERE IT STANDS**, without consuming it — so
  `[C"Am"E]` is a chord of one, an annotated `E`, and a leftover `]`, **which is an INVISIBLE
  BARLINE**.
- **A KEY SIGNATURE'S POSITION IS A TABLE, NOT A SHIFT** — three brackets on the clef's
  `verticalPos` with per-accidental exceptions, one of which IS the tenor. ⚠️ The exceptions
  key on the WRITTEN NAME, case included, which is fixed by the TREBLE position.
- **A CHORD'S DOTS MAY COINCIDE**; **A BELOW ANNOTATION HANGS OFF THE LYRIC LANE** (⚠️ and
  its RESERVE is off the INK while its BASELINE is off the LANE, or the lane counts twice);
  **A PERCUSSION CHORD RESOLVES ITS HEAD GLYPH PER PITCH**, and the STEM rule reads the
  HIGHEST head's.
- **A MID-MEASURE `[K:… clef=]` IS NOT THE LINE'S CLEF** — it leaves `staff.clef` alone,
  draws where it stands, and reaches the NEXT line's staff. The projection, the drawing and
  the `%%MIDI drummap` stamp all take that split now.
- **A CHORD-HEAD SLUR CLOSE TAKES ANY OTHER OPEN** when its own position is empty, which is
  what makes `(F [GB)d]` a curve where `[(CE)G]` is two halves.
- **A CURVE CROSSING A SYSTEM IT DOES NOT END ON GETS A FULL-WIDTH ARC** at `startx + 10` to
  `lineEndX`, at abcjs's own `startY = endY = above ? 14 : 0`.
- **`%%abc-copyright` AND ITS SIBLINGS DRAW ROWS**; the span's `restOfString` is what follows
  `%%abc`, so the `abc` counts ONCE.

**AND THE FOURTH PASS FOUND NOTHING**, which is the other honest outcome: six more shapes,
every one byte-exact on its first run, six more entries turned from prediction into
measurement. That is the signal that sent the fifth pass at abcjs instead.

## THE RULES THIS SESSION PAID FOR

⚠️ **ENUMERATE THE REFERENCE, NOT THE NOTES.** A `ponytail:` ledger is a list of what WE
wrote down; abcjs's switch is a list of what IT has. The second found sixteen defects in an
afternoon after the first had gone quiet.

⚠️ **A "SAME" IS ONLY AS GOOD AS THE SHAPE THAT ASKED.** Thirty-one directives were measured
inert on a plain tune and seven of them move a tune that can reach them. When a probe says
nothing changed, ask what the control could not express.

⚠️ **A SOURCE READ THAT PREDICTS AN ABSENCE IS STILL A PREDICTION.** "abcjs has no `stem`
element in any of its three voices" was written from the source and denied by one dump.

⚠️ **AN ARITHMETIC IDENTITY IS NOT AN ARITHMETIC EQUALITY.** `(w - m) - m`, `x * STEP /
STEP`, `2 * m` versus `m + m` — three of this session's regressions were re-associations
that are exact in algebra and not in doubles. The default path must keep its original
grouping; only the new case may take the new one.

⚠️ **A MODULE-LEVEL GLOBAL IS THE PREVIOUS RENDER'S UNTIL IT IS ASSIGNED.** Reading
`PAGE_PADDING` before its assignment took the last tune's margins and broke a byte-exact
fixture.

⚠️ **AND A GATE THAT CANNOT BE REGENERATED STOPS GROWING WITH THE CORPUS.** The lines gate
read zero for a week while five fixtures were outside it. Before trusting a green gate, ask
whether its ORACLE covers what the corpus now holds.
