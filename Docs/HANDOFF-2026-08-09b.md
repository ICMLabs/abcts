### 1. THE TIMING JOIN'S OTHER TWO SURFACES

`setTiming`'s TIME half is done (0 of 38). Two surfaces remain and neither is a
continuation of it:

- **The GEOMETRY half has no oracle at all.** `left`, `endX`, `top` and `height` are on
  every row and abcjs's own `timing.test.js` asserts none of them. A control ladder against
  its rendered SVG is the only route, and that is a gate to BUILD, not a table to read.
- **`currentTrackMilliseconds` is stamped by the FLATTENER, not by `setupEvents`**
  (`abc_midi_flattener.js:530-540`) — a third surface, and the two `elements` cases in
  `tests/corpus-timing/` are already harvested and waiting for it. A note reached twice
  through a repeat carries `[3000, 9000]`: an ARRAY where a once-played note carries a
  number.

### 2. The SVG DOM contract — `visual/svg.test.js`, `svg-per-line.test.js`

Eight portable cases. They assert `[data-name=…]` presence and group structure, which no
geometry gate covers and `abcts/compat` explicitly promises.

### 3. The dynamic glyphs — the last measured Bravura leak in strict

`DYNAMIC_CEILING = 0.94` in `tests/decoration-x.test.ts` is the number and the upgrade path.
The ANCHOR is exact; only the outline is Bravura's. CLAUDE.md's audit ruling says any
Bravura input reachable in strict is a defect, and this one is now measured rather than
asserted.

### 4. THE STRUCTURAL PASS — step 1 done, the rest queued

Split `layout.ts` along its seams; close the `ENGRAVE` bare-literal table; the module-level
mutables if they are a real hazard. **NO BASELINE MAY MOVE** — if one does, that is a
behaviour change; revert it rather than re-record it. And do not "clean up" the comments:

### 5. THREE THINGS THAT NEED LANCE, NOT CODE
