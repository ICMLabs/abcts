### THE ONE LEFT — `options-01-fonts`, MEASURED TO THE DIGIT AND NOT LANDED

A `P:` label's baseline: **ours 382.82, abcjs 382.81.** Probed, and it is not a measurement
at all — it is a GROUPING:

    ours       (t.y + pad) * PX + oy                    382.81500000000005 -> "382.82"
    abcjs      (rung + oy) + size + pad                 382.815            -> "382.81"

`renderText` sets `hash.attr.y = y`, then `+= hash.font.size`, then `+= hash.font.padding`,
then rounds ONCE (`draw/text.js:28-31`, `:57`, `:63`) — three adds onto the PAGE's y. We
build `rung + size + pad` in the layout and add the staff origin at emit. **AN ASSOCIATION
IS A DECISION**, and `(382.815).toFixed(2)` is `"382.81"` where the longer double is
`"382.82"`.

⚠️ **THE REGROUPING WAS WRITTEN AND REVERTED, AND WHAT IT EXPOSED IS THE REAL WORK.**
Emitting `(t.y - t.size) + oy + t.size + pad` assumes every non-centred music text stores
`rung + size`. It does not: **`PlacedText.centered` is set at ONE site and abcjs has FOUR**
— `draw/voice.js:19` (the voice name, ours), plus `draw/brace.js:92` (a BRACE's voice name),
`draw/relative.js:49` (a text decoration), `:52` (a pitched annotation) and
`draw/triplet.js:12` (the tuplet number). The three headless goldens that moved —
`zocharti-loch`, its `-classes` twin and `wrap-04`'s `Violin II` — are all brace-owned voice
names taking the wrong branch, and `wrap.test.ts` moved two wrap explanations with them.

**So flag the other four first, then regroup.** Do not scope the regrouping to the part
label to make one fixture green.

⚠️ **DO NOT CARRY THE WEBKIT PLAYBOOK STRAIGHT OVER.** WebKit is at ZERO, so every one of
these is something only Blink's metrics express. A rounding boundary is the shape to expect,
and a rounding boundary is not fixed by measuring harder — it is fixed by matching abcjs's
ARITHMETIC, which is a different search from matching its FONT.
